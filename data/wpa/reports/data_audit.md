## State variables

- **9. Position is not recorded directly — it must be inferred.** Inference rules (already implemented and validated in `scripts/analysis/parse_bout_pbp.py`): period 1 and every OT start neutral; the `Choice N` column sets the next period's start (Bottom/Top/Neutral chosen by one wrestler, the other takes the complement); Takedown and Reversal put the scorer on top; Escape returns both to neutral. Near falls, penalties, stalling and cautions do not change position. Not visible: a restart after an out-of-bounds or an injury, and a wrestler 'cutting' an opponent (that shows up only as the opponent's Escape).
- **10. Riding time, NCAA:** running riding time is **not** stored as a field. 54.0% of bouts have scorekeeper riding-time notes, and 33.6% have at least one note pinned to a clock time. The end-of-match 'Riding Time' point appears as its own event in 48.0% of bouts.
- **10. Riding time, Conference:** running riding time is **not** stored as a field. 11.4% of bouts have scorekeeper riding-time notes, and 9.3% have at least one note pinned to a clock time. The end-of-match 'Riding Time' point appears as its own event in 44.7% of bouts.
- Running riding time can be **reconstructed from position** (who is on top, from the moment of a takedown/reversal/top choice until the next escape/reversal/period end) — `parse_bout_pbp.py` does this. Checked against every clock-pinned scorekeeper note: **7,895 of 8,326 (94.8%) agree within 5 seconds.** This is reconstruction from the event stream, not from the final riding-time value, so it doesn't fall under the spec's 'do not reconstruct running riding time from final values' rule — but it is a decision for TJ (see open questions).
- **11. Period choice, NCAA:** recorded. 99.9% of bouts that reached period 2 have a `Choice 1` column; 99.9% of bouts that reached period 3 have `Choice 2`. The toss winner is the first entry in `Choice 1` (either their pick or 'Defer').
- **11. Period choice, Conference:** recorded. 99.4% of bouts that reached period 2 have a `Choice 1` column; 99.2% of bouts that reached period 3 have `Choice 2`. The toss winner is the first entry in `Choice 1` (either their pick or 'Defer').
  - NCAA choice for period 2 (n=6,399): bottom 49.1%, defer→bottom 40.3%, defer→neutral 7.0%, neutral 1.6%, defer→top 1.3%, top 0.8%
  - NCAA choice for period 3 (n=6,128): bottom 72.2%, neutral 23.2%, top 4.6%
  - Conference choice for period 2 (n=7,872): bottom 52.2%, defer→bottom 37.6%, defer→neutral 6.6%, neutral 2.0%, defer→top 1.0%, top 0.7%, +4 0.0%, +1 0.0%, -2 0.0%
  - Conference choice for period 3 (n=7,352): bottom 73.6%, neutral 22.8%, top 3.6%
- **12. Overtime:** present, labelled `Overtime 1..N`. Sudden victory vs tiebreaker is told apart by label and by the official result type (`SV-1`, `TB-1`, `TB-2`, `UTB`). The table above shows the OT format changing by season (from earlier work: **2026 is the only season under the current OT rules**).

### 14. How falls and tech falls end the event stream

- **Falls are not logged as an event.** The stream simply stops. Of 1,615 falls with play-by-play, the last period column present is: Period 1 828, Period 2 449, Period 3 298, Overtime 1 29, Overtime 3 6, none 5 (a period with no events or notes has no column at all, so this is only a lower bound on where the fall happened). The fall's official time (elapsed match time) is in the results: NCAA `score` holds it as M:SS (655 of 655 NCAA falls), conference results in `time` (960 of 960). *Corrected 2026-09-29 during step 2: the first version of this audit said NCAA results had no fall time — they do, in `score`.* So a fall is a terminal jump at its official time.
- **Tech falls, E1 2015 (TD 2, NF 2/3):** final margin distribution {15: 12, 16: 9, 17: 7, 18: 2}; outliers {-16: 1} (negative = a swapped-sides bout the loaders already correct; a single 14 = one record, likely a scoring correction). The scoring event that reaches the threshold is the last one logged.
- **Tech falls, E2 2016–23 (TD 2, NF 2/4):** final margin distribution {15: 209, 16: 126, 17: 63, 18: 28, 19: 7}; outliers {14: 1} (negative = a swapped-sides bout the loaders already correct; a single 14 = one record, likely a scoring correction). - **Overtime:** sudden victory 1:00 → 2:00 in 2022; 2026 is the only season under the current OT rules (earlier work). Keep OT out of the regulation table, as the spec says.

**Depth and source are not constant.** Ranks 1–33 come from a different source by era: FloWrestling (2023+, depth = however many Flo published, often 24–33, ELO below that), actual NCAA placement + committee seed (2019–2022), placement + real seeds ≤16 + ELO (2015–2018). **Leakage risk:** in 2015–2022 ranks 1–8 *are* the NCAA placement, and the 2023+ Flo snapshot used is the final (often post-NCAA) one — both are decided after the conference tournament. For a conference match that means the 'strength' input partly encodes results from two weeks later. The spec accepts end-of-season values for a retrospective model; flagging it because it will flatter validation on conference data.

## Section 11: open questions, answered

1. **Timestamp quality / usable matches:** good. Exact seconds, explicit periods, a few dozen clock reversals across ~15k bouts. 84.7% of NCAA and 84.2% of conference bouts have a clock on every regulation scoring event.
2. **Running riding time:** not recorded as a field, but reconstructable from the event stream (94.8% within 5 s of the scorekeeper's clock-pinned notes). **Period choice:** recorded (toss winner, defer, and each wrestler's pick for periods 2 and 3) in >99% of bouts that reach those periods.
3. **Conference matches with both wrestlers ranked (top 33):** 34.2% (2,913 of 8,530); about a third have one ranked, a third neither.
4. **Share of match-moments in thin cells:** answered by the sparsity audit (step 4), not here.
5. **Separate tables per rules era:** three eras exist in the data. Likely answer: pool E1 with E2 (one season) and keep E3 separate or as a smoothing slice; the sparsity audit decides.

### Decisions needed from TJ before step 2

- **A. Riding time:** use the reconstruction from position (validated ~95%), or follow the spec's fallback (drop `rt_status`/`rt_bin`, final riding-time point only)? Recommendation: use the reconstruction; the spec's prohibition is about back-filling from the *final* value, which this doesn't do.
- **B. Bouts with an unclocked scoring event (~15%):** drop them from the state table, or place each unclocked event between its clocked neighbours? Recommendation: place them (the neighbours usually pin it within a few seconds, e.g. a near fall right after its takedown), and flag those bouts so validation can check them separately.
- **C. Pre-2019 NCAA seeds 17–33** are a random draw: treat as unseeded (tail value). Recommendation: yes.
- **D. Rules eras:** E1 (2015) pooled with E2, E3 separate. Recommendation: yes, pending the sparsity audit.
- **E. Conference rank leakage:** end-of-season rank includes later NCAA results. Accept (the spec allows it) but report conference validation with that caveat, or restrict conference to Flo seasons 2023–26? Recommendation: accept and caveat; it only affects the step-10 second pass.
- **F. Falls have no logged time** in the play-by-play. The terminal jump is placed at the last logged event. Recommendation: accept; it only affects where on a WP curve the final jump is drawn, not WPA of earlier events. *(Superseded in step 2: every fall has an official time — NCAA `score`, conference `time` — so falls end at their real time; the last-event rule is only the fallback for injury defaults and DQs.)*
