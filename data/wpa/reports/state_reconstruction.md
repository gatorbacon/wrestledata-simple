# WPA step 2 — state reconstruction

Generated by `scripts/wpa/build_states.py` (shared definitions in `scripts/wpa/wpa_common.py`). Inputs are the same play-by-play and official results as the data audit. Nothing in `data/` outside `data/wpa/` is changed.

**Outputs** (`data/wpa/states/`), every bout stored once from the winner's side (`w` = winner, `l` = loser); step 3 builds both perspectives:

- `{kind}_bouts.csv` — one row per bout: ids, `era`, raw and usable seeds (`w_seed`/`l_seed`, decision C), result, how regulation ended, riding-time checks, flags (`n_scoring_time_estimated`, `max_scoring_gap`, `position_conflicts`, `unknown_position_secs`, `score_check`).
- `{kind}_events.csv` — one row per event with the state before (`b_*`) and after (`a_*`): `t_rem`, `period`, score, `margin`, `pos` (`neutral`/`w_top`/`l_top`/`pending`), `choice`, `rt_diff` (winner minus loser, seconds), `rt_status`. Includes the break events (`toss`, `defer`, `choose`), `regulation_end` (where the riding-time point is applied) and overtime events (`section = ot`).
- `{kind}_samples.csv` — the state every 10 s of regulation (`t_rem` 420, then 415, 405 … 5: the middle of every 10-second bin, so the last 10 seconds are sampled too), for spec 3.1's fixed-cadence samples. A bout that ended early (fall, tech fall, injury, DQ) has samples only before it ended.

## Riding-time lock function (spec 2.3)

`wpa_common.rt_status(rt_diff, t_rem)` — exactly the spec's simple bound; threshold `rt_diff >= 60` (1:00 or more, confirmed by the near-the-line table above: the point goes to the leader from 60 s up, not below). Position is not used to tighten the bound yet (spec: refine only if validation shows it matters).

| A's advantage | Time left | Expected | Got | Case |
|---:|---:|---|---|---|
| 55 s | 6 s | live | live | spec example: 55 s up, 6 s left -- A can still reach 1:00 |
| 55 s | 4 s | locked_none | locked_none | spec example: 55 s up, 4 s left -- A can't reach 1:00, B can't either |
| 60 s | 0 s | locked_in | locked_in | exactly 1:00 at the end earns the point (>= 60) |
| 59 s | 0 s | locked_none | locked_none | 0:59 at the end does not |
| -60 s | 0 s | locked_out | locked_out | mirror of the 1:00 case |
| 75 s | 10 s | locked_in | locked_in | 75 s up, 10 s left: worst case 65 |
| 75 s | 16 s | live | live | 75 s up, 16 s left: B could ride it back to 59 |
| 0 s | 420 s | live | live | opening whistle: everything reachable |
| -30 s | 25 s | locked_none | locked_none | 30 s down, 25 s left: neither side can reach 1:00 |

Mirror check: 20,000 random states viewed from both wrestlers — `mirror(A's view) == B's view` (margin, position, choice, riding time, lock status, riding-time bin) failed **0** times.

## Bins (for step 3)

`margin_bin` clamps to ±15; `t_bin` = 10-second bins of regulation time left (0–41); `rt_bin` = 10-second riding-time bins truncated toward zero, clamped to ±6 (±60 s), so a mirrored state always lands in the mirrored bin.
