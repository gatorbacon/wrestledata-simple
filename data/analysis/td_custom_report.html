3dPins by seed gap: is it the takedowns or the better wrestler?

NCAA Championships only (conference results carry no seeds). Matches with a takedown differential that ended in a pin, both wrestlers seeded. Seed gap = the seed of the wrestler behind on takedowns minus the seed of the wrestler ahead: +32 means the 1 seed led on takedowns against the 33 seed; −32 means the 33 seed led against the 1 seed. Bars show how often the takedown leader got the pin, in buckets sized so each holds a similar number of pins.
