HOW TO READ THIS REPORT
  - 'Bouts' = wrestler-bouts. Most scenarios count BOTH wrestlers in a bout, each from their own
    point of view, so a scenario like 'scored nearfall points' can include the winner and the loser
    of the same bout. 'Won' = how many of those wrestlers won the bout.
  - Every table has two blocks: ALL BOUTS, and DECIDED BY POINTS ONLY. 'Decided by points' means
    the official result was a decision, major decision, tech fall, or a sudden-victory / tiebreaker
    overtime. It EXCLUDES pins (falls), forfeits, injury defaults, disqualifications, and 56 bouts
    whose official result could not be matched.
  - Takedown differential = takedowns scored minus takedowns allowed in the bout (overtime
    included). Reversals, escapes and nearfall are NOT takedowns.
  - 95% CI = Wilson 95% confidence interval on the win %. Small samples have wide intervals; don't
    read much into gaps that fit inside each other's intervals.
  - A pin is not logged as a scoring event, so 'nearfall points' only counts nearfall that was
    actually awarded points before the match ended.

DATA NOTES
====================================================================================================
  - Bouts with no play-by-play (609) are byes (opponent 'Unknown'), forfeits and injury defaults,
    and are left out.
  - Official results come from the team scrapes (mt/processed_data/ncaa_men), extracted by
    build_conference_results.py and matched to each bout by last name + first initial + weight
    (Timmy/Timothy McCall are the same wrestler): 2,389 of 2,445 bouts matched (97.7%). Unmatched
    bouts (56) count in ALL BOUTS but not in DECIDED BY POINTS. The usual reasons are a wrestler or
    event missing from a team scrape, or a rematch that can't be paired.
  - A swapped-sides bout is detected against the official score (winner's score lower than the
    loser's and equal to the official score reversed). For unmatched bouts only the impossible
    case, a winner trailing by 15+, is caught.
  - Header scores in the play-by-play file sometimes differ from the official score by 1 point
    (usually the riding-time point); those bouts are still matched.
  - Only tournaments that TrackWrestling has, and that were scraped complete, are included - see
    the coverage table. Known real gaps: Big Ten 2020, Big 12 2016-18, MAC 2019/2021/2023, Pac-12
    2015-17 and 2024-25, SoCon 2026. (Official results for some of those tournaments exist in the
    team scrapes and are saved in parsed/matches.json, but there is no play-by-play to analyze.)
  - Play-by-play sides/scores that were swapped in the source file and corrected here:
      * 2021 Big Ten 285: Christian Lance (Nebraska) defeated Tate Orndorff (Ohio State) (file says 2-3, official 3-2)
      * 2021 Pac-12 141: Real Woods (Stanford) defeated Angelo Martinoni (CSU Bakersfield) (file says 2-3, official 3-2)
      * 2022 Big Ten 285: Michael Woulfe (Purdue) defeated Zach Schrader (Maryland) (file says 0-2, official 2-0)
      * 2023 Pac-12 184: Jarad Priest (Cal Poly) defeated Mason Diel (Little Rock) (file says 6-8, official 8-6)
  - Falls are not logged as events, so scoring context (e.g. nearfall) before a pin may be incomplete.
