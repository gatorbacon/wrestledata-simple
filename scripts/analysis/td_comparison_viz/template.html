Takedowns and winning: how consistent is the pattern?

The same four analyses, run on the NCAA Championships and on six conference tournaments (Big Ten, Big 12, ACC, MAC, Pac-12, SoCon), across four time windows. The question in every chart: does the pattern hold from one window to the next, and does it hold in both kinds of tournament?

How overtime was settled

Sudden victory (Overtime 1) is first score wins; the tiebreakers (Overtime 2 and 3) give each wrestler a 30-second turn on bottom. Only 24–62 overtime bouts per cell, so single percentages are noisy.
