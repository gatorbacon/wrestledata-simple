DPG — Dual Points Gained. Your dual-point result (DEC 3, MD 4, TF 5, F 6) minus what a typical wrestler gets against that same opponent. Beat a #10 by major and you get a lot. Tech a #200 and you get a little. Season DPG is the average of those matches, not a total.
