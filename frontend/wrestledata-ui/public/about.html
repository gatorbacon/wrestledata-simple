About MatSavant

MatSavant is an analytics platform for college wrestling, built to measure individual impact and team value using match-level results.

Core Metrics

  • Match Index (MI) – a per-match impact metric that measures how much value a wrestler adds per match relative to a replacement-level Division I starter, adjusted for opponent quality. It separates record from performance and captures the difference between being undefeated and wrestling like the best guy in the room.
  • xTP – Expected team points at the NCAA Tournament based on advancement, placement, and bonus

What This Is (v1)

  • Individual and team profiles
  • MI and xTP leaderboards
  • Match-level impact analysis
