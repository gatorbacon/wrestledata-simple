🧪 NCAA Bracket Archive

Every NCAA wrestling championship since the first one in 1928: the bracket for each weight, the placewinners and the team scores.

1928–2012 come from the wrestlingstats.com bracket sheets; 2013 on from TrackWrestling. The format changed many times (Bergman wrestle-backs, bad-point rounds, and consolation brackets that grew from finalists' victims to every loser), so each year is shown in its own format.
